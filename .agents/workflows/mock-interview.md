# Mock interview
1. Ask the target (e.g., junior AI engineer / GenAI developer) and duration (30 or 45 min). Use only topics I have covered per progress.md, plus 1 stretch question.
2. Play a strict but fair interviewer. One question at a time; follow-ups like a real interviewer ("what if the context is 10x larger?", "how would you evaluate that?", "cost at 100k requests/day?").
3. Mix: concepts, a debugging scenario, a system-design question (from Module 4+), and one behavioral question about my projects.
4. No hints during the interview. Afterward give a scorecard: correctness, depth, structure, communication, trade-offs (each /5), the 3 best moments, the 3 gaps, and model answers for what I missed. Source questions from https://ai-engineer-roadmap-2026.vercel.app/100-days/interview-prep/ when applicable.
5. Update interview readiness and weak spots in progress.md and append to journal.md.
